## Playable scope

- C09 returns to the ancient temple and establishes the fourth support altar; C10 and each protagonist's ending are playable through the real story controller and combat engine. The active protagonist handles the central boss while the other four handle distinct support altars. The high priest's established backlash death and five POV knowledge boundaries remain.
- The first central checkpoint is a bounded holdout, not an impossible damage gate or an automatic victory. The four support cuts persist independently. The final combat requires an actual victory, with death/retry and same-save post-ending exploration preserved. Boss coefficients are authored candidate tuning, not a claim of final global balance.
- A dedicated invasion-leader sprite and separate open/closed central ritual backgrounds are wired only to the corresponding story encounters. Ordinary regional backgrounds and other protagonists' art are preserved.
- Thirty additional personal events: dwarf 8, human 6, elf 6, goblin continuation 4, Ashen 6. Sixty credible choices, 120 site-local interactions and eight real local battle objectives. These are authored narrative interactions, not a spatial simulation. Seventeen early events have distinct present-day late alternatives. Existing regional 30 NPC / 60 events and the earlier six sibling events are counted separately.
- Personal progress can be paused; late entry does not replay historical rescue. Late dwarf entry may start P03 without granting missed P01/P02 rewards. Goblin continuation waits for the actual C10 family safety confirmation and uses the safe camp rather than the old household location.
- Twenty-five small/stage reward claims use fixed equal-choice budgets. Each completed full personal line grants one slot-selectable ordinary non-set Q3 item. Level is current hero level ±2 at first claim. Slot/seed/level are durably reserved before result generation and bag handling; retries cannot switch slot or reroll. This reward is not granted merely for the six early family moments or for mainline completion alone.

## Verification and remaining limits

The historical regression suite has been repaired against approved current rules: current save keys, authentic historical provider fixtures, current equipment/tree counts, correct image retry mock URLs and scoped old-source boundaries. Meaningful assertions remain; no production compatibility layer was added for retired saves.

Final integrated command receipts and the release dependency graph must be recorded after the last runtime change. Earlier baseline results (229 MJS and 87 CJS plus aggregate npm success) are not by themselves the final candidate's acceptance.

Natural resource-constrained simulations and actual earned-save routes demonstrate viable five-race progression, foreign dungeon unlocks and mainline completion. See PROGRESSION_ECONOMY_V069.md. Simulated engine hours are not measured human playtime. Potion use varies greatly with healing and lifesteal; the old 40–80 / four-hour target is not a universal result and no artificial minimum or healer nerf was added.

Actual browser/mobile layout acceptance remains unverified: managed preview startup timed out; the owner-private live URL presented a login gate. Fake-DOM tests and native raster compositions are separately labelled and do not stand in for browser verification.

Infinity Gauntlet acquisition/crafting foundation is on a separate gated branch, not included in this candidate. Active-skill counting and unique-product disposal proposals await the owner's response. Its art/prototype does not constitute a shipped item. AI fusion and cross-save unlocks remain unapproved future concepts.
