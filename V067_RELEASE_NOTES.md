# v0.67 — Expanded local cast, playable invasion and temporary world wars

Base: exact live v0.66 `ddfcc1993a38574a2631916519d94e861c674b9f`.

## Delivered scope

- Thirty named local NPCs now host sixty authored local events, in addition to the five Return Ring material scenes. The original fifteen identities and thirty event definitions, IDs, choices, rewards and save records are preserved exactly. All thirty NPCs have distinct transparent 96px portraits; only the current area's cast is requested. Ring postings reuse the same person's portrait.
- Forty local events are one-offs and twenty repeat. Repeat exchanges spend two ordinary Q0 materials for one other ordinary Q0 material; repeat patrols require one new genuine victory for one ordinary Q0 material. Successful cycles retain the five-minute cooldown and manual reacceptance. No rare-material or gold reward inflation was added. Five new exploration objectives count genuine post-acceptance gameplay entry, not old map records, previews or mainline staging/encounters. Historical offline receipts use the actual entry time; current acceptance/reporting still enforce the live war gate.
- The remaining C07 scenes and C08 are playable for all five POVs through actual controller dialogue, source-gated knowledge and engine encounters. Each POV keeps its own hero. Elf seal layers one and two are genuine winnable battles; the third is a real seal-core encounter resolved only by the explicit, exact-target old-wedge action. The protected tool is consumed once in the same validated transaction as that scene's completion. Quota, stale target and repeated submission do not consume it or fabricate a receipt.
- The canonical C08 invasion completion ends the full Elf–Ashen **story** territory lock, after the invasion, successful ritual and priest-backlash death facts are recorded together. Winning the encounter alone, elapsed time, confession and the preceding interruption do not end it. Opposing ordinary travel and NPC activities resume, but an unrelated random war remains independent. This is a shared threat, not reconciliation.
- C07–C08 use dedicated transparent priest, seal-core and invasion-wave artwork. The native production renderer and roster use those exact identities, not unrelated heroes/creatures. Roster errors use bounded automatic retry and manual recovery without issuing a combat-target action.
- Random wars use a separate deterministic saved RNG stream: 1% chance at each eligible ten-minute real-time checkpoint, duration 20–60 whole minutes, with a strict one-hour maximum. Only one random incident is active globally. Reload/offline time counts toward expiry. Offline catch-up processes at most 24 pending checks and the bounded four-hour combat window, not months of retroactive rolls. Newer autosave/health timestamps do not erase an unprocessed checkpoint. Rollback safely expires random locks instead of extending them; it never ends a story war. This is a local-clock policy, not network-clock anti-cheat.
- Existing admitted fights may finish; locked enemy territory cannot supply another ordinary field, dungeon, boss or farming battle. Home/neutral regions remain available. Mandatory foreign story access is limited to exact registered scene/site/objective bindings; it never opens ordinary NPC or farming access. Withdrawal remains explicit.
- Equipped Return Ring victory healing is now **15% of final maximum HP**, capped at maximum, once per successful settlement including dungeon stages. It ignores potion/healing multipliers. The existing eligible-channel +3 percentage-point drop bonus, zero/empty source rules, unique-held gems and once-per-journey craft remain unchanged. Infinity-gem base rates from v0.66 are unchanged.
- A narrow validator fix accepts the already-authored Human boss 1.2 / 220ms next-basic slow, alongside the existing 1.15 / 165ms effect. It does not alter boss stats, combat cadence or rewards.

## Safe interruption and saved progress

Camp rest and the existing loadout retreat atomically cancel only the current unfulfilled registered encounter binding, preserve earlier scenes/receipts, restore the scenario's pre-entry HP policy and leave the seal tool unconsumed. The ordinary voluntary-rest cost is unchanged. Retrying creates a new encounter ID; an old target cannot complete it. Failed storage keeps the old encounter and binding together. Pausing and reloading do not silently cancel a fight.

Schema 6 and `ashencrown-v6` are unchanged. No reset or development-generation migration is introduced. Valid current optional story/NPC/Ring/diplomacy state remains supported. C05's real Ashen core grant and subsequent normal equipment/loadout use remain intact. v0.66 image recovery/newer-save protection and v0.65 Goblin facing are retained; skill-tree scrolling/layout was not changed.

## Explicit content boundary

**C09–C10, the return to the temple, four support altars and final battle are not implemented in this release.** The complete written screenplay is not a claim that the complete mainline runs in the game. Post-C08 UI states this boundary; no final-ending flags are fabricated. Separate newly requested hero/sibling art work is not part of this candidate.
