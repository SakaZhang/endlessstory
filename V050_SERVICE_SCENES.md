# v0.50 — forge and merchant scene art

## Scope
- Replace the normal fusion-page forge canvas and normal merchant canvas with original layered pixel environments matching the battle art direction.
- Preserve actual HTML controls: 1–5 fusion inputs, one result, costs, quality risk, paid pending claim, eight merchant offers, quoted inventory and purchase/refresh transactions.
- Use 480×160 logical scenes at a full 3:1 aspect ratio, nearest-neighbor rendering. Scene decoration contains no clickable controls or baked prices/text.
- Load only the requested scene; show an honest loading/error state and retry. No fallback to the old geometric forge/merchant drawings.
- No combat engine, RNG, scheduling, schema 3, save key, currency, item or probability change. No additional animation loop.
