Exactly SIX separate full body poses of SAME nonhuman faceless obsidian crystalline rift sentinel, angular floating head with thin teal slit, asymmetric jagged dark armor, violet crystal shoulder, long dark spear with teal crystal tip. Readable finely dithered pixel art, hard pixel edges, limited dark teal violet palette, serious proportions, not chibi, no human face. Layout strict 3 columns x 2 rows on TRANSPARENT background, evenly sized cells with huge separation. All six FACE LEFT (enemy on right attacks hero on left). Top row: idle standing with spear pointing down-left; anticipation twisting torso and pulling spear backward to right; attack deep leftward lunge spear extended horizontally LEFT. Bottom row: recovery bent stance withdrawing spear; hurt torso recoiling RIGHT from hit on left, one arm lifted; death collapsed crystalline body in a low pile with broken spear horizontal, recognizable armor. Six genuinely distinct authored poses, same body scale in every cell. Every sprite fully contained in its cell, wide weapon fully visible. No effects clouds or large glow halo, no floor, no shadows, no labels, no grid, no text. Sprite bodies designed for about 110 pixel display height with thick readable silhouette. Do not include any other characters. Actual alpha transparency.

Keep each figure's feet at same cell-relative floor line, including death pile at same floor. No shadows, no text, no grid. Crisp pixel art, no glow outside silhouette.
