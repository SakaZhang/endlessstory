# v0.44 — A03 pixel battle extension (candidate)

Adds five exact A03 identities: oathless guard, inscription lizard, broken tablet idol, tablet executioner and Morl the oathkeeper, plus their oath-ruins background layers. Normal A03 encounters, dungeon elite and boss use the same underlying combat and source IDs; Morl is not presented as the dwarf king. No story trigger is added.

The two shield-only enemy actions remain non-damaging presentations. The display bridge consumes their actual shield/cast output, not a fabricated punch or damage number. Real casting, status, stable target IDs, death and shield overlays are tested in the normal App path.

A01/A02 sprite assets, mother/juvenile mapping, all mechanics and save/RNG behavior are retained. A04–A10 remain whole-scene legacy fallback: this batch covers 13/44 canonical monster IDs, not all dwarf art. The separately developing later-area assets are not included.

Native canvas/DOM integration tests are not full-page browser QA. Publication requires final independent gate and root authorization.
